# ⚡ Power Electronic Converters for Electric Vehicle Systems using MATLAB & Simulink

*3-Day Student Development Program — Certification*

A structured training program on power electronic converter design and simulation for Electric Vehicle (EV) systems, conducted using MATLAB & Simulink.

**Status:** ✅ Completed (Certified)

## 📋 About the Program

A 3-day Student Development Program on **"Power Electronic Converters for Electric Vehicle Systems using MATLAB & SIMULINK"**, organized by the Department of Electrical & Electronics Engineering, Rao Bahadur Y. Mahabaleswarappa Engineering College (RBYMEC), Ballari, in association with the **New Age Innovation Network (NAIN)**.

- **Duration:** 16th – 18th March 2026
- **Venue:** EEE Department, RYMEC, Ballari
- **Resource Person:** Mr. Shivaraj V S, Abeyaantrix Edusoft
- **Organized under:** Dr. Kotresh S (HOD-EEE) & Dr. Rajasekaran Shanmugam (Principal, RYMEC)

---

## 🎯 What the Program Covered

- Fundamentals of power electronic converters used in EV systems
- Simulation of converter topologies using MATLAB & Simulink
- Practical exposure to modeling power electronics circuits for electric vehicle applications
- Hands-on sessions and activities on converter design and analysis
